part 9.4.1.b.

Explain why there is not a way to complete \(\{\vi,\vi, ???\}\) to be a right-handed coordinate system.
🔗
Solution.
Because both vectors are in the same direction, it is not possible to specify a right handed orientation. You can’t curl your fingers in the same direction they are extended in the right handed orientation definition.
