Activity 9.8.5.

(a)

Write a couple of sentences describing the graph of \(r=c\text{,}\) where \(c\) is a constant. You should consider what values of \(c\) will correspond to different shapes.
🔗
Solution.
The graph of \(r=c\) is a circle of radius \(|c|\) centered at the origin.

(b)

Write a couple of sentences describing the graph of \(\theta=d\text{,}\) where \(d\) is a constant. You should consider what values of \(d\) will correspond to different shapes.
🔗
Solution.
The graph of \(\theta=d\) is a line through the origin that makes an angle of \(d\) with the horizontal axis. This graph should be both directions of the line because you would need to consider both positive and negative values of \(r\text{.}\)
