part 10.2.1.b.

Write a couple of sentences to describe both geometrically and algebraically what happens to the output of \(\vr_8(t)\) as \(t\rightarrow\pi\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
As the input \(t\) gets closer to \(\pi\text{,}\) the output of \(\vr_8(t)\) will approach the vector \(\langle -1,0 \rangle\) where the y-component approaches from below (less than 0) and the x component approaches from above (greater than -1).
