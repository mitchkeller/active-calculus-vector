(f)

Find the length of each of line segments in the plot that is your answer to part 9.1.1.e. Explain how the right triangle idea you drew can be generalized to find the distance between two points \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
The length of the vertical segment, shown in blue, is 6. The length of the horizontal segment, shown in red, is 8. The length of the diagonal segment, shown in black, is 10 and can be calculated with a pythagorean theorem like argument.
🔗
In general the length of the vertical segment between \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\) will be \(\abs{y_2-y_1}\text{.}\) The length of the horizontal segment between \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\) will be \(\abs{x_2-x_1}\text{.}\) The length of the diagonal segment between \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\) will be \(\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}\text{.}\)
