part 10.5.3.b.

For the given parameterization of the ellipse, find \(\vv(t)\) and \(\va(t)\text{.}\) Also identify the parameter values that correspond to the points of the ellipse with the largest and smallest \(x\) or \(y\) coordinates.
🔗
Solution.
Derivatives of \(\vr(t)\) will give \(\vv(t) = \langle -a \sin(t), b \cos(t) \rangle \) and \(\va(t) = \langle -a \cos(t), -b \sin(t) \rangle \text{.}\)
🔗
The parameter values with \(t=0\) and \(t=\pi\) have the largest curvature and \(t=\frac{\pi}{2}\) and \(t=\frac{3\pi}{2}\) have the smallest curvature.
