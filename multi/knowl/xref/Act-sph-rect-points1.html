part 9.8.10.a.

Use the conversion equations between rectangular and spherical coordinates to find the rectangular coordinates of the following points:
🔗

(i)

\((\rho, \theta, \phi)= (3, \frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{2})\)
🔗
Solution.
\((3\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\left(1),3\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)\left(1),3\left(0))=\left(\frac{3}{2},\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2},0\right)\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(ii)

\((\rho, \theta, \phi)= (4, \frac{4\pi}{3}, \frac{3\pi}{4})\)
🔗
Solution.
\((4\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right),4\left(-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right),4\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right))\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(iii)

\((\rho, \theta, \phi)= (\sqrt{7}, 20^\circ, \pi)\)
🔗
Solution.
\(\left(4\cos(20^\circ) (0),0,-\sqrt{7}\right)=(0,0,-\sqrt{7})\)
