part 9.6.2.d.

Give parametric equations for the line \(\mathcal{L}\) passing through the point \((2,0,2)\) and perpendicular to the plane \(p_3\) described by the equation \(x+2y-2z = 7\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
The normal vector of the plane \(p_3\) is \(\langle 1, 2, -2 \rangle\text{,}\) which will be the direction of line \(\mathcal{L}\text{.}\)
🔗
The corresponding parametric equations are \(x(t) = 2 + t, y(t) = 2t, z(t) = 2 - 2t\text{.}\)
